Trading Insights

In the markets, success isn't about discovering truth—it's about clearly defining your strategy and making it a truth through consistent refinement.

Core Axes of Market Worldview

Optimism vs. Pessimism

Markets can rise or fall, and each move invites a belief: "this is opportunity" vs. "this is danger." Neither is wrong.

What matters is recognizing which lens you're using—and adjusting size, strategy, and timing accordingly.

The Adaptation Spectrum

Some traders imprint their style on the market. Others mold their style to the market's current character.

Both approaches work—if aligned with skill, conviction, and risk management. The key is clarity: know your stance and don't switch mid-trade.

Continuation vs. Reversal

Every trend is either nearing its end or just beginning. Both assumptions are valid—but never at the same time.

Select your bias before entry, define your trigger for confirmation, and execute without second-guessing.

Trading Strategy Framework

Core Framework

Inputs

1. The exact millisecond enter is executed.
2. The exact millisecond exit is executed.
3. The direction of the order.
4. The size of the order.
5. Time.

Outputs

1. Win Rate (W).
2. Reward-to-Risk (RR = Avg Win ÷ Avg Loss).
3. Capital (C).

Core Equations

Expectancy (per $1 risked)

E = W × RR − (1−W)

E ≥ 0.2 (to account for friction)

Position Size (per trade)

Size = (C × f) / Distance to stop

Optimal Fraction (Kelly)

f* = W − (1−W)/RR

Most use 1/2 Kelly (we're emotional creatures)
